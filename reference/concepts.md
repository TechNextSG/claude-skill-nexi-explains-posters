# One distinct poster concept per post

Starting points drawn from each episode's own world (`episode-worlds.md`): no archetype appears twice in this list.
Before building, watch the episode's contact sheet, sharpen the concept, then run `uniq.py --check` with all 9 axes
(the register is the law, this list is a plan). Night scenes are few on purpose (EP00, EP15, Halloween): the owner
prefers light, warm posters. Nexi's costume = the one she wears in that episode.

Columns: **Archetype** (poster genre) · **Moment** (the frozen frame) · **Camera** · **Light** · **Words go** · **Signature device**.
