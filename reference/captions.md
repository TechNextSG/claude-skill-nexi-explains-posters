# Captions for episode posters

One `captions.md` per delivery folder, one section per poster (final version only). Per poster: files, premiere,
what is on the image, when to post, then one caption per platform (never the same text everywhere).

## Facebook
```
<Hook: the episode's moment in one line, 1 emoji max>

<Nexi Explains EPnn "Title" premieres on YouTube on <Day date> at 6 PM SGT. One line on what happens.>
✔ <what the viewer learns 1 (app/service in plain words)>
✔ <2>
✔ <3>

<Next episode or series line>

🔔 Set a reminder: <youtube link>
All episodes: https://technext.asia/nexi-explains

#NexiExplains #Odoo #TechNext #<topic> #<topic>
```
## Instagram
2-4 short lines, "Reminder link in bio", 10-20 hashtags in one block at the end (always #NexiExplains #TechNextAsia).
## LinkedIn
900-1,300 characters: the business problem the episode is about, what the episode shows (one concrete detail from the
poster), what TechNext does, one question to invite comments, both links, 3 hashtags. No emoji walls.

Facts in captions come from episode.py and the episode itself; samples (Lotus Mart, Bluebay Trading ...) are called
samples or "a little shop called ..." , never presented as clients.
