# Nexi Explains: every episode's own visual world

Research for one unique cinematic promo poster per episode (2026-10-06). Read-only on all sources.
Sources: `tn-wt-explains/_src/nexi_explains.py` (titles, hooks), `nexi-tutorial/web/films/*.js` (header comments, wear() calls, painter names), `nexi-tutorial/deliver.py` (file mapping), the two posting plans, the thumbnail contact sheets (S1 `00 Thumbnails - contact sheet.jpg`, S2 `contact-sheet-16x9.jpg` for EP00-04, `00 Quick Tips S2 - thumbnails.jpg`, the three holiday YouTube thumbnails).
"?" = not stated in the sources. Cells are shortened from the film headers; facts only.

## Quick notes for the designer

- Nexi is a white egg-body robot with a dark visor face, star/round/heart/X eyes, and a costume = a hat (17 hats exist in `nexi-core.js buildWear`: hardhat, conductor, pilot, glasses, strawhat, beret, chef, cap, knight, bowtie, goggles, bellhop, beanie, tophat, boppers, helmet, astro). The hat is the quickest way to tell the episodes apart.
- S1 episode thumbnails are ALL TechNext blue with yellow title text (the episodes "keep the TechNext blue"). S1 Quick Tip thumbnails and S2 Quick Tip thumbnails each have their own bright gradient. S2 episode thumbnails (EP00-04 only so far) are dark blue with an orange/yellow starburst and one shocked cast face. Holiday thumbnails are purple / red / navy.
- Samples in all films are fictional and labelled ("sample company", "Sample data"): Bluebay Trading, Lotus Mart, Kopi Co., Lintel & Co., Manila Trading, S00042. No Malaysia anywhere, no prices, no cyber security.
- S2 cast at Bluebay Trading: Maria (Accounts, Excel expert, dry humour), Ben (Warehouse lead, 58, slow and steady), Jun (Sales, young, eager), Mr. Lim (the Boss, loves shortcuts). Recurring S2 critters: Spike the pantry cactus, Mr. Lim's green parrot, the rubber chicken, Ben's tortoise, Biscuit the puppy.
- S2 EP14-EP23 (Arcs 4+5) are registered in `deliver.py` but POSTPONED and are not in `nexi_explains.py`; they are not catalogued here.
- File mapping (deliver.py `EPISODES`): EP01 walk, EP02 apps, EP03 beforeafter, EP04 myth, EP05 erp, EP06 accounting, EP07 sales, EP08 inventory, EP09 reorder, EP10 crm, EP11 custom, EP12 enterprise, EP13 odooai, EP14 automation, EP15 chatbot, EP16 web, EP17 social, EP18 brand, EP19 appdev, EP20 iot, EP21 network, EP22 finale. Quick Tips: tip.js = QT01, tip2..tip34 = QT02..QT34.
